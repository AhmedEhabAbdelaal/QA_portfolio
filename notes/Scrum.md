## Scrum defintion

* A framework that help teams/organizations to generate solutions for complex problems. It is a lightweight framework, theory/philosophy on how to acheive a certain goal and helps the team generate value.Scrum shows the efficency of the current managment, enviroment & work techniques, so if there is a room for improvement it will be held.


## Scrum theory 

* Scrum is founded on the knowledge and the experiance of people (empiricism --> tgrobat) 

## Scrum Pillars 

1. Transparency: The currne tproces/word must be visible to all those who is working on it. without transparency inspection is misleading and wasteful

2. Inspection: the progress toward the goal must be frequantly inspecte to detect potential problems or changes.Inspection enables adaptation. Inspection without adaptation is considered pointless. Scrum events are
designed to provoke change.

3. Adaptation: an action must be taken when the process or the output is deviated from the wanted goal (this is called adaptation). A self-managing or not empowered people will have hard time in adaptation.

## Scrum Team

* the fundamental unit of scrum is a small team containning:
    - 1 scrum master 
    - 1 product owner 
    - developers

* it's best that a team is a small 10 or less to apply suscceful communication between them if the team gets larger then multiple scrum team should be considred , they all share the same Product goal,Product Backlog, and Product Owner.


## Developers 
* They are the people in the scrum team that are commited to create usable increment in each Sprint

## Product Owner 
* This person communicates clearly the product needs and the requirments in order the scrum team to work on it transperantly which result in maximizing the product value 


## Scrum Master 
* this person communicates the guidelines of the Scrum concept across his team members and insure everyone is maxmizing their output whoever the role of this person in the team (product owner or developer)

## Scrum Events 
* Sprint is the proces where the idea truned into value, it has a fixed length events of one month or less to keep consistency

* during the sprint, No changes will be executed if it will put the sprint goal in danger, Quality doesn't decrease and a clarified negotiation must take place with the product owner if the scope is changed since there are more things is leraned from the Sprint

* Long Sprints are invalid ( since with longer time span between each one may increase complexity or deviation from the goal ), so short span Sprints (month or less) are best to generate more learning cycles and limit risk to waste time and resources
