## Scrum defintion

* A framework that help teams/organizations to generate solutions for complex problems. It is a philosphy, theory on how to acheive a certain goal.Scrum shows the efficency of the current managment, enviroment & work techniques, so if there is a room for improvement it will be held.


## Scrum theory 

* Scrum is founded on the knowledge and the experiance of people (empiricism --> tgrobat) 

## Scrum Pillars 

1. Transparency: The currne tproces/word must be visible to all those who is working on it. without transparency inspection is misleading and wasteful

2. Inspection: the progress toward the goal must be frequantly inspecte to detect potential problems or changes.Inspection enables adaptation. Inspection without adaptation is considered pointless. Scrum events are
designed to provoke change.

3. Adaptation: an action must be taken when the process or the output is deviated from the wanted goal (this is called adaptation). A self-managing or not empowered people will have hard time in adaptation.

## Scrum Team

* the fundamental unit of scrum is a small team containning:

* it's best that a team is a small 10 or less to apply suscceful communication between them if the team gets larger then multiple scrum team should be considred , they all share the same Product goal,Product Backlog, and Product Owner.
